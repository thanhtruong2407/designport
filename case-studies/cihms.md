# CiHMS Case Study

## Overview

CiHMS was a hospitality management platform (PMS) with 35+ modules. The work focused on improving usability, consistency, and workflow quality across a large operations-heavy system used by hotel teams.

## Role

UX/UI Designer

## What I Worked On

- Improved the design system to ensure consistency across modules
- Designed key areas including Front Office, Housekeeping, Inventory, Maintenance, and Channel Management
- Conducted research with hotel staff to identify usability issues
- Translated business requirements into user flows and interface designs
- Improved UX flows and reduced UI inconsistencies

## Design Focus

The challenge was scale and operational complexity. The platform covered many workflows, so the design work needed to make the system more coherent without losing the specificity of each module.

## Why It Matters

CiHMS is a strong example of enterprise workflow design, large-system thinking, and cross-functional problem solving in a domain where consistency and operational clarity matter every day.

# Banking And Fintech Platforms Case Study

## Overview

This work covered digital banking products for SME and retail users, with a focus on complex financial workflows such as money transfers, payments, and related service experiences.

## Role

UX/UI design, user research, journey mapping, cross-functional collaboration

## What I Worked On

- Conducted user interviews to understand SME and retail banking needs
- Designed user flows, wireframes, and core financial-service features
- Worked with stakeholders to align solutions with business needs and financial regulations
- Supported localization for the Vietnamese market
- Contributed to design-system and UX improvements across web and mobile products

## Design Focus

The main challenge was handling complex, high-stakes workflows in a way that remained clear and trustworthy for users. That meant paying close attention to flow logic, domain constraints, and coordination with business stakeholders.

## Why It Matters

This project is one of the strongest examples of domain complexity in my background. It shows research, workflow design, stakeholder alignment, and product thinking in an environment where usability and clarity directly affect user confidence.

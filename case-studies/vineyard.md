# VineYard Case Study

## Overview

VineYard was a recruitment technology platform centered on dashboard design, hiring workflows, and data visualization. The work focused on making information easier to interpret and helping users navigate core product tasks more clearly.

## Role

Product Designer

## What I Worked On

- Wireframing and prototyping dashboard flows
- Designing UX/UI patterns for recruitment-related workflows
- Structuring data visualization for easier scanning and decision support

## Design Focus

This project required balancing information density with usability. The dashboard needed to support practical hiring tasks without becoming visually noisy, so the work emphasized clarity, structure, and workflow alignment.

## Why It Matters

VineYard is a strong example of B2B product thinking, especially in designing interfaces that support operational tasks and data-heavy decision making.

# Renkus-Heinz Case Study

## Overview

Renkus-Heinz involved redesigning audio management software to improve clarity and usability. The product had complex controls and a technical context, so the design challenge was to create a more structured and understandable experience.

## Role

Product Designer

## What I Worked On

- UX/UI redesign work
- Wireframing and interface restructuring
- Design-system thinking to improve consistency

## Design Focus

The key challenge was reducing complexity without losing functionality. The redesign emphasized interface clarity, more coherent structure, and a smoother experience for users working with advanced audio-management tasks.

## Why It Matters

This project highlights system thinking and practical redesign work in a complex software environment, where usability improvements depend on both visual clarity and better interaction structure.

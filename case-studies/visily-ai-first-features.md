# Visily AI-First Features Case Study

## Overview

This work focused on AI-first product features inside Visily.AI, an AI-powered UI design tool. The project area covered feature definition, AI behavior training, output validation, and research into interaction patterns that could make AI-driven design workflows more usable and scalable.

## Role

Product Designer with an AI product focus.

## What I Worked On

- Defined and clarified AI design feature directions based on user needs
- Participated in training AI behavior for UI design-related tasks
- Evaluated generated outputs for usability, consistency, and design quality
- Explored chat-centric interaction patterns for AI-assisted workflows
- Researched design-system and token-structure approaches to support scalable UI generation

## Product Perspective

The most valuable part of this work was not only designing interfaces, but helping connect three moving parts: user expectations, AI capabilities, and product behavior. That meant validating outputs, finding where AI responses broke design logic, and helping shape a more useful workflow for end users.

## Why It Matters

This case study best represents my transition toward product-oriented work. It combines UX, AI behavior evaluation, collaboration with engineers, and practical thinking about how to improve product outcomes through iteration.

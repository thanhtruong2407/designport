# Home

## Structure

```text
Hero -> Introduction
Works -> Proof
Experience -> Credibility
Skills & Certifications -> Capability
About -> Context
Contact -> Conversion
```

---

## 0. Navigation

**Detail elements:**

- Logo: Thanh truong
- Menu: Works; Experience; Skills; About; Contact

**Requirements:**

- Remains sticky at the top of the screen for persistent navigation
- Click the logo to return to the homepage
- Menu items enable smooth scrolling to relevant sections
- Includes default and active states for navigation clarity

---

## 1. Hero

**Purpose:**

Introduce who you are and attract attention immediately.

**Detail elements:**

- Name: Trương Huỳnh Việt Thanh + Title (Product Designer -> Product Owner)
- Profile image
- Short value proposition (1-2 lines)
- CTA buttons (View Works, Contact)
- Quick highlights (years of experience, number of projects, domains such as AI, Banking, SaaS)

**Requirements:**

- The `View Works` CTA directs users to the Works section
- The `Contact` CTA directs users to the Contact section
- `Việt Thanh` links to the About section for quick access to background information

---

## 2. Works

**Purpose:**

Show proof of your capability. This is the most important section.

**Detail elements:**

### Featured Projects (3)

- Thumbnail
- Project title
- Role
- Short description (impact-focused)
- Tags (AI / System / Fintech...)
- CTA: View case study

### Other Projects (3-5)

- Project title
- Short description
- Tags
- CTA: View case study, shown on hover

**Requirements:**

- Hover interactions provide visual feedback through subtle thumbnail and card animations, reinforcing the primary CTA
- Clicking a project card reveals detailed project information in a pop-up

---

## 3. Experience

**Purpose:**

Show your career journey and credibility.

**Detail elements:**

- Company name
- Role
- Timeline
- Key contributions focused on impact, not tasks
- Optional metrics / outcomes

---

## 4. Skills & Certifications

**Purpose:**

Show how you work and what you are strong at.

**Detail elements:**

### Skill tag rows (3 rows, no number/title/description)

- Row 01: AI Feature Development · System Prompting
- Row 02: Feature Ownership · Project Coordination · Stakeholder Communication · Delivery Tracking
- Row 03: UX Audit · User Research · Design Systems · Product Specifications

### Certifications

- Name
- Organization
- Year

---

## 5. About

**Purpose:**

Provide context about you after the visitor already trusts your work.

**Detail elements:**

- Short story (Designer -> Product Owner transition)
- Your strengths (bridge design, business, AI)
- Your focus (AI product / fintech)
- Core competencies

---

## 6. Contact

**Purpose:**

Convert a visitor into an opportunity.

**Detail elements:**

- Name + Title
- Email
- Phone number
- LinkedIn link
- Portfolio link
- Location

**Requirements:**

- Highlight the name, email, and phone number
